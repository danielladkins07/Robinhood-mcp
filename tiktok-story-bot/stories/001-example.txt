AITA for refusing to give up my window seat to a guy who "always sits there"?
So I started a new job last month, and on day one my manager told me to grab any empty desk. I picked the one by the window. Nobody was sitting there. No stuff on it. Nothing.
Three weeks later, a guy named Derek walks up, drops his bag on my keyboard, and says, "You're in my spot."
Turns out Derek works remote four days a week. He comes in on Thursdays. And apparently, for two years, the window desk has been "his Thursday desk."
I told him I didn't know, and he was welcome to any of the other empty desks. He said, and I quote, "I don't do fluorescent lighting."
I stayed put. He complained to our manager. Our manager, who clearly didn't want to deal with it, said we should "work it out like adults."
So I proposed a deal. Derek can have the desk on Thursdays, if he takes over my Friday reports. He laughed. I didn't.
Here's the twist. He said yes.
It's been six weeks. I work from the break room every Thursday, Derek gets his sunlight, and I haven't written a Friday report since.
My coworkers are split. Half say I'm petty. The other half want to know if Derek has any other desks he's attached to.
So, AITA?
